# Contributing to passforge

First of all, thank you for taking the time to contribute!

passforge is a personal project, and I'm currently the sole maintainer. While I'm not accepting pull requests, I truly value your feedback, bug reports, and feature suggestions. Your input helps shape the future of the tool.

---

## How can I contribute?

### Reporting bugs

If you find a bug, please open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tu-usuario/passforge/issues).

To help me understand and reproduce the issue, please include:

- A clear and descriptive title.
- Steps to reproduce the behavior.
- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened (include error messages if any).
- Your operating system and passforge version (`passforge --version`).

### Suggesting enhancements

Have an idea for a new feature or an improvement? I'd love to hear it!

Open an issue and describe:

- The problem you're trying to solve.
- How you envision the feature working.
- Any alternatives you've considered.

Even if you're not sure about the implementation, just sharing the idea is helpful.

### Asking questions

If you have a question about usage, installation, or anything else, feel free to open a discussion or an issue with the label `question`.

---

## Pull requests

At this time, I'm **not accepting pull requests**. I prefer to implement new features and fixes myself to maintain a consistent vision and code quality.

However, your suggestions and reports are always welcome and will be taken seriously for future releases.

---

## Code of conduct

Please be respectful and constructive in all interactions. I'm committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

---

## Thank you!

Every contribution, no matter how small, is appreciated. Thank you for helping make passforge better!
